                                                    病人資料申請人注意事項 
                                                                                             Note of Application –  
                                                     Request for Release of Medical Information 
  
1. 本申請是根據《個人資料(私隱)條例》而進行。任何個人或代表個人的有關人士有權提出查閱資料及資料複本要
求。 
This request is processed under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.  An individual or a relevant person on behalf of 
an individual is entitled to make a Data Access Request to ascertain whether hospital holds the personal data of the Data 
Subject and to be supplied with a copy of such data. 
 
2. 請將申請表連同所需證明文件一併傳真至：(852)  2840 1986；或電郵至：gen.off@canossahospital.org.hk；或寄至
「舊山頂道一號，嘉諾撒醫院總務部」。待回覆後，申請才作實。 
 
辦公時間：星期一至星期五 09:00 – 17:00；星期六 09:00 – 12:30；星期日及公眾假期休息 
 
Please send the application form and all relevant supporting documents by fax to: (852) 2840 1986; or e-mail to: 
gen.off@canossahospital.org.hk; or mail to “Canossa Hospital General Office, No. 1, Old Peak Road” by post.  Application 
will be valid upon our confirmation.  
 
Office Hours: Monday to Friday 09:00 - 17:00; Saturday 09:00 - 12:30; Sundays and public holidays CLOSED 
 
3. 請出示身份證明文件 (身份證/護照)。 
Please provide identity document (HKID / Passport). 
 
4. 如非申請人親身申請或領取資料，其父/母/合法監護人/獲授權人士須出示以下文件： 
If applicant cannot apply or collect the data in person, Parents / Legal Guardian / Authorized Person needs to bring: 
a. 申請人的身份證明文件副本(身份證/護照) Copy of Applicant’s identity document (HKID / Passport) 
b. 申請人的出生證明書副本 (適用於父/母/合法監護人) Copy of Applicant’s Birth Certificate (For Parents / Legal 
Guardian only) 
b. 獲授權人士的身份證明文件副本 (身份證/護照) Copy of Authorized Person’s identity document (HKID / Passport) 
 
5. 於下列情況下申請表的第五部份必須填寫 : 
Part 5 of the application form must be completed under the following circumstances: 
(i) 18 歲以上資料當事人授權第三者代為領取其紀錄。 
Data subject aged 18 or above authorizes third party to collect the requested data on behalf of him / her.  
(ii) 如資料當事人未滿18 歲，其父/母/合法監護人必須填寫此部份。 
This section must be completed by parents / legal guardian if data subject aged below 18. 
 
6. 本院會在確認收到申請費用後的40 個工作天內向申請人作出書面回覆。 
Hospital will reply to the applicant within 40 working days upon confirmed receipt of the payment. 
 
7. 所有醫療報告及病人資料均以英文書寫，而本院提供的資料將截至申請當日為止或由負責醫生決定。 
All medical reports and patient’s information are written in English.  Information provided will be up to the request 
received date or up to doctor’s decision on the relevancy of the case. 
 
8. 若申請人於被通知可以領取資料後的三個月內仍未領取，有關資料將會被銷毀，恕不另行通知。 
If the applicant does not collect the requested data within 3 months after being notified it is ready for collection, the 
requested data shall be destroyed without prior notice. 
 
9. 所有的醫療紀錄均按照醫院管理局和醫院保留指引保留。 
All medical records are retained in accordance with the Hospital Authority (HA) and Hospital retention guidelines. 
 
10. 本院將根據最新之價目表收取申請費用。一旦提出要求將不會退還費用。 
Application fee will be applied according to Hospital’s current price list.  No refund of charge will be made once a request 
is made.
