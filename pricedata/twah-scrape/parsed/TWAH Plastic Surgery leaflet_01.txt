What Is Plastic Surgery?  什麼是整形外科?
整形外科是一門專為因先天性缺陷 (如兔唇裂顎)、外傷及燒傷、
疾病 (如乳癌、皮膚癌切除) 和年紀老化而導致容貌及外觀受損
和改變而設的專科，利用整形手術，改善及美化外表，使病人
回復舊觀，恢復正常的儀容。
整形外科中的美容科，是專為美化容貌 (如雙眼皮、隆鼻手術)、
體形及外表 (如抽脂、隆胸手術) 的專科。
愛美是人類的天性，整形外科能助以上的病人，改善他們容貌
及外觀上的缺失，使他們重建信心，達致美好幸福的生活。
Plastic Surgery is a special branch of surgery that deals with alternation 
in appearance and facial features as a result of congenital anomalies 
(e.g. Cleft lip, Cleft palate), trauma, burns, disease (e.g. breast cancer, 
skin cancer) and aging.  Utilizing techniques of plastic surgery, the 
altered appearance can be improved and restored.  Cosmetic Surgery 
in plastic surgery concerns about beautifying and improving the facial 
features (e.g. double eyelid surgery, nose reshaping) and body contour 
(e.g. liposuction, breast enlargement).
It is natural for people to desire to be beautiful, plastic surgery can help 
the above patients to improve their outer appearance, so that they can 
regain conﬁdence and have better quality of life．
