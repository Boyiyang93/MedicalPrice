Important Information  手術須知
+ Inform your doctor if you suffer from an allergy, high blood pressure, 
heart disease, diabetes and if you are taking anti-thrombosis 
medication.
+ Normal vision will be restored within a few weeks following the 
surgery. Mild refractive errors are normal (including presbyopia). 
+ Eyedrops may be prescribed postoperatively as an aid to healing 
and prevention of infection. Follow-up consultations are important 
and should not be postponed.
+ Take plenty of rest after the surgery and avoid vigorous exercises 
and swimming.
+ Avoid rubbing or pressing on your eye.
+ Consult the doctor immediately if you develop persistent pain, 
redness, swelling, or any loss of vision.
+ 如患有嚴重或慢性疾病，如高血壓、糖尿病、心臟病、藥物
敏感、抗凝血藥物服用者等，應於手術前告知有關醫生。
+ 患者一般會在手術後數星期內逐步改善視力，可能有輕微屈
光不正（包括老花），此屬正常情況。
+ 手術後須連續使用眼藥水數星期，直至完全康復，並須遵醫
囑覆診。
+ 康復期間應多休息，避免做劇烈運動或游泳。
+ 手術後應避免揉眼，以免影響傷口癒合。
+ 如眼睛突然感到劇痛、紅腫、視力驟降，應立即向主診醫生
求助。

Causes and Symptoms   成因和病徵
Cataracts generally occur in older patients.  Diabetes, ocular trauma, long 
term usage of steroids and an inherited predisposition, represent some 
of the risk factors. At onset, a cataract may not cause obvious symptoms, 
but as it increases in size and density, the symptoms become troublesome.
Signiﬁcant symptoms:
+ A clouding or blurring of vision
+ Colors tend to fade 
+ Eyes tire easily
+ Photophobia (discomfort, and at times pain, due to glare and bright light)
糖尿病、外來創傷、藥物、先天遺傳等都有可能導致白內障，
但以老年白內障最為常見。早期的白內障可以沒有明顯徵狀，
但隨著病情惡化，患者可能會出現下列病徵：
+ 事物變得模糊不清
+ 對色彩、光暗的敏感度降低
+ 眼睛容易疲倦
+ 畏光
Risks Associated with Cataract Surgery  手術風險
A few patients may develop infection, glaucoma, or a problem with 
wound healing, however, the overall incidence of these complications 
is small, and the majority of patients are very satisﬁed. Please discuss 
any concerns you have with the doctor prior to committing to 
surgery.
白內障手術的成功率達九成以上，大部份患者對其效果感到滿
意，唯少數情況有可能出現如細菌性發炎、青光眼、傷口癒合
等問題，請於手術前向眼科醫生查詢。
Phacoemulsiﬁcation is the most advanced method and utilizes an 
ultrasonic probe to soften and break up the lens, thus allowing the lens 
with its cataract to be removed by suction.
Implantation of an intraocular lens restores the patient’s vision. This 
plastic lens requires no after-care, and becomes a permanent part of 
the eye.
手術摘除是最有效治療白內障的方法，可局部或表面麻醉後進
行。香港港安醫院—荃灣備有先進儀器，可為患者進行微切口
白內障手術。手術簡單且傷口細小，相較於傳統手術可較快
復原。
利用嶄新的超聲波乳化儀器，乳化和抽出白內障。
植入人工晶體有助患者恢復視力，且一般而言，植入人工晶體
屬永久性，無須清洗或替換。

What is Cataract?   甚麼是白內障
Cataracts are one of the three main causes of blindness in Hong Kong. 
It is not necessary to wait until the cataract becomes mature. When 
there is a signiﬁcant decrease in vision that affects your work or daily 
activities, cataract extraction should be encouraged. Surgical risks 
increase as the cataract matures.
A cataract is a clouding of the eye's natural lens. It blocks the refraction 
of light to the retina and causes deterioration of vision. Cataract can 
develop into acute glaucoma, causing headaches and pain in the eye. 
It is important to consult your doctor if any of the above symptoms 
should occur.
白內障是香港三大致盲殺手之一。許多人以為要待白內障「成
熟」了以後方能施手術摘除，其實不然。當它影響到工作或日
常生活時，患者就需考慮動手術，以減低疾病對生活的影響，
提高生活質素。而且，當白內障已變「硬」和「成熟」，手術
風險亦會相對提高。
白內障是指眼球內的晶體變得混濁，無法折射光線至視網膜，
令視線變得模糊，視力下降。要留意的是，白內障有機會演變
為急性青光眼，引起頭痛和眼痛。如遇此情況，必須立即接受
治療。
