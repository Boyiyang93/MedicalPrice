癌症是香港的頭號殺手，據統計數據，每年約有三成死亡個案
源於癌症。良好的生活和飲食習慣有助預防，時刻留意早期癌
症徵兆，定期檢查身體，有助及早發現這些殺手。早期診斷加
上治療，可以得到更佳的治療效果。
香港港安醫院—荃灣配備先進診斷儀器，集合了富經驗的專業
醫護團隊，致力提供體貼的癌症諮詢、風險評估、檢查、診斷、
治療和護理服務，與您一起維護健康。
Cancer is one of the leading causes of death in Hong Kong, and 
according to statistics, approximately 30% of deaths each year’s can 
be attributed to cancer. Good lifestyle and eating habits are helpful in 
the prevention of cancer, along with regular checkups and being aware 
of the early signs of cancer. Early diagnosis improves the chances of 
successful treatment and better outcomes. 
Equipped with advanced diagnostics and a team of experienced medical 
professionals, Hong Kong Adventist Hospital – Tsuen Wan is committed to 
providing attentive and comprehensive services in cancer consultation, 
risk assessment, examination, diagnosis, treatment and care.
