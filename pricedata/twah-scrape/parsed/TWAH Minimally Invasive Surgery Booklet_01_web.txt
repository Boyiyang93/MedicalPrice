Feature 特點
What Is Colorectal Cancer? 認識大腸癌
Colorectal Cancer usually starts from the growth of polyps in the large bowel. When a polyp 
develops over time, it may turn into cancer.  Colorectal Cancer is the most common cancer 
in Hong Kong, 5036 new cases are recorded which is the highest among cancers. Over 90% 
of colorectal cancer cases occurs in people aged 50 or above.
大腸癌的雛型，就是腸道內的一顆顆瘜肉。當瘜肉隨年月而增長和病變，便很大機會惡化
成大腸癌。大腸癌是香港最常見的癌症，當中超過九成患者都是五十歲以上人士。
First Step: Colorectal Cancer Screening 第一步：大腸癌篩查
Faecal Occult Blood Test can detect hidden blood in stool samples, even in tiny amount. The 
presence of hidden blood indicates bleeding somewhere in the bowel, the patients would be 
advised to take colonoscopy examination.
大便隱血篩查能檢測肉眼難以察覺的大便帶血症狀。如大便帶血，即腸道已出血，患者求
診時，醫生會按需要安排進行大腸鏡檢查仔細檢驗。
Follow up: Colonoscopy 跟進：大腸鏡檢查
It is an investigatory procedure that a doctor uses a long ﬂexible tube to examine the inside 
of the large bowel and polyps could be removed by surgeon immediately. To detect 
colorectal cancer earlier, colonoscopy is currently a reliable and safe way.
醫生會將一條長而柔軟的導管放入患者體內，詳細檢查大腸，如醫生在過程中發現瘜肉，
也可即時切除。定期進行安全及可靠的大腸鏡檢查，發現早期病變，可有效地預防
大腸癌。
Treatment 治療
Oncologist will participate in the treatment. Treatments include surgery, chemotherapy, 
radiotherapy or targeted therapy.
大腸癌患者一經確診後，臨床腫瘤科專科醫生會參與治療。治療方法包括手術、化療、電
療和標靶治療。

HDTV Video Processor  高品質HDTV圖像處理系統
The Olympus CV-290 unit is the world’s ﬁrst high deﬁnition (HDTV) video processor for 
endoscopy. Coupled with the Olympus GIF Type HQ 290 endoscope, the system has 
dramatically improved image quality.
高品質圖像處理技術為現時全球最尖端的內視鏡診療系統，配合內視鏡，可得出清晰影像，
令診斷和治療更加準確。
Narrow Banding Imaging  NBI窄頻內視鏡檢查
Its principle is to exclude the red light which has a long wavelength, leaving only the blue 
and green lights which have rather short wavelengths and are able to reveal blood vessels 
and surface characteristics in high contrast images. Subtle changes in both mucosal structure 
and color are highlighted. It works like a ﬁlter which detects early abnormalities by 
spotlighting potential malignancies with remarkable clarity and detail.
原理為去掉波長較長之紅光，留下波長較短的藍光和綠光，病灶表層的微細血管變化更易被
觀察。即使過去難以察覺的黏膜組織和顏色變異，都可以清楚顯現。其目的在於偵測早期病
灶，有助準確地檢視病變或癌變位置。
Laparoscopic Colectomy  微創腹腔鏡大腸切除手術
Laparoscopic colectomy, a minimally invasive surgery for colorectal cancer, is considered as 
an alternative to traditional open colectomy. Surgeons will use surgical instruments to 
remove a portion or entire colon. Compared to open colectomy, laparoscopic colectomy 
brings less pain, reduced blood loss and shorter recovery time to patients.
腹腔鏡手術屬微創手術，外科醫生會使用特別設計的外科手術儀器切除部分或全部大腸。與
傳統手術相比，微創腹腔鏡大腸切除手術能減少病人的術後痛楚、減少出血量和康復較快。

When is Endoscopy Indicated? 什麼情況下需要接受內視鏡檢查？
Gastroscopy 胃鏡檢查
Gastroscopy is performed to examine your upper gastrointestinal tract for either the purpose 
of diagnosis, or to monitor healing. Common diseases include：
+ Ulcers - of the esophagus, stomach, or duodenum; Occasionally these ulcers may be 
malignant
+ Tumors - of the stomach or esophagus 
+ Inﬂammation - of the lining of the upper gastrointestinal tract (esophagitis or gastritis)
+ Suspected intestinal bleeding
+ For patients who have symptoms such as difﬁculty in swallowing, upper abdominal pain, 
heart burn or indigestion
胃鏡檢查有助確診或治療下列的上消化道疾病：
+ 潰瘍 – 包括食道、胃、十二指腸，潰瘍有機會是癌變的先兆
+ 腫瘤 – 包括胃或食道 
+ 炎症 – 包括食道炎或胃炎
+  懷疑腸道出血
+  吞嚥困難、上腹持續疼痛，胃灼熱或消化不良
Colonoscopy 大腸內窺鏡檢查
A colonoscopy is an examination of the large intestine. The examination is an important tool 
for the early detection and treatment of cancer in the large bowel. It also enables diagnosis 
of various other diseases of the lower gastrointestinal tract:
+ Colon and rectal cancer - a serious but usually curable malignancy
+ Polyps - ﬂeshy tumors which may be forerunners to colon cancer
+ Inﬂammation of the colon (colitis) which may be acute, chronic, or recurrent
+ Diverticulosis and Diverticulitis - pockets along the intestinal wall that can become 
infected
+ Suspected bleeding in the large bowel
+ Abdominal symptoms, such as pain or discomfort, chronic diarrhea, constipation, or a 
change in bowel habits
結腸內視鏡有助確診和治療大腸的早期病變或癌症，還有助檢查下消化道多種疾病，包括：
+ 結直腸癌 – 較嚴重但有機會治癒的癌症
+ 瘜肉 – 瘜肉可能是大腸癌的先兆 
+ 大腸發炎（急性、慢性或持續復發）
+ 憩室病及憩室炎 – 腸壁向外凸出成袋狀，可引致感染和發炎
+ 懷疑大腸出血
+ 腹部持續疼痛或不適、持續腹瀉、便秘或大便習慣無故改變
