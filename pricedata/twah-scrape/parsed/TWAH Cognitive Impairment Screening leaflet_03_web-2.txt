What is Cognitive Impairment?
甚麼是認知障礙症？ 
Cognitive impairment is quite common in Hong Kong and is typically 
used to describe various symptoms caused by the loss of brain cells, 
such as memory loss and impaired judgment. Although there is 
currently no cure, early detection and treatments can delay cognitive 
decline. Therefore, undergoing a regular diagnostic (cognitive) check 
is very important. Using advanced equipments, our experienced 
specialists in Neurology at the Hong Kong Adventist Hospital – Tsuen 
Wan can help you to assess your cognitive abilities and ensure the 
health of your brain. 
認知障礙症（又名「腦退化症」）在香港相當普遍，泛指腦部退
化，導致記憶力下降、判斷力減低。雖然此病症仍然無法根治，
但是，盡早接受治療可以延緩功能衰退。因此，檢查至為重要。
香港港安醫院—荃灣的腦神經科醫生經驗豐富，結合先進儀器，
助您評估認知能力，為腦部健康把關。
認知障礙症主要分成兩大類：
+ 阿爾茲海默氏症
 此類患者最為普遍，腦部功能逐漸退化，惟成因未明。 
+ 血管性認知障礙症
 腦中風或其他血管疾病破壞腦部功能，病情隨血管問題惡化
 而加劇，呈梯級性衰退。

Non-drug Treatments  非藥物治療
Drug Treatments  藥物治療
醫藥界目前未有根治認知障礙症的良方，藥物治療僅針對腦部
衰退的症狀，調節行為，安定情緒，減慢病情惡化。
Currently, no medications have been shown to cure cognitive 
impairment. Medications are only used to treat symptoms of brain 
decline, regulate behavior, stabilize the emotion and delay the progression 
of the disease.
These type of treatments occur through simple activities and training 
to improve the patient's mood, behavior and cognitive ability. Brain 
functions can be maintained and even improved.
+ Alzheimer’s disease
 Patients in this category are the most common. Symptoms include  
the gradual degradation of brain functions but the causes are still 
unknown.
Detection and Its Importance  檢查方法及其重要性
藉着簡短智能測驗、身體檢查（包括血液測試、腦部掃描等），
醫生能判斷認知障礙症的病情。末期患者缺乏自理能力，對家
人及照顧者構成壓力。及早診斷並進行治療，可保持患者的認
知及活動能力，從而提高患者生活質素，減輕身邊人的負擔，
故認知障礙症檢查具有關鍵性作用。
+ Vascular cognitive impairment 
 This type of cognitive impairment is caused by strokes or other  
cerebrovascular diseases which damage the brain. The patient’s  
condition deteriorates with the cerebrovascular problems. It follows  
a “stepped” progression, with symptoms remaining at a constant 
level for a period of time and then deteriorating suddenly. 
With short aptitude tests and physical examinations (including blood 
tests, brain scans, etc.), doctors can determine the condition of 
cognitive impairment. Terminal patients lack the ability to take care of 
themselves and this imposes a great burden on their families and 
caregivers. However, early diagnosis and treatment can maintain the 
patient's cognitive ability and mobility to improve the quality of life 
which help reduce the burden on people around them. Therefore,  
an early diagnosis of cognitive impairment is crucial.
