Reminders

+ You are suggested to wear comfortable outﬁts on the assessment day.

As our lifestyles continue to evolve, the prevalence of chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, 
high blood pressure, and cancer among younger patients has gradually increased. Receiving regular 
physical examinations ensures that we are consistently aware of our health condition and risks, so 
that we may adjust our lifestyle accordingly to prevent disease. Even in the event of a diagnosis of 
illness, early and appropriate treatment will signiﬁcantly improve the chances of recovery.
Hong Kong Adventist Hospital – Tsuen Wan is a leading medical center offering one-stop, specialist 
services in New Territories West. Our interdisciplinary medical team provides ﬁrst-rate examination, 
diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation services supported by state-of-the-art equipment such as 
advanced PET/CT, mammography, ultrasound, cardiac catheterization, and MRI systems. As your 
healthcare partner, Hong Kong Adventist Hospital – Tsuen Wan is committed to walking alongside 
you in your journey of health by providing you with the best, most comprehensive physical examination 
programs.
