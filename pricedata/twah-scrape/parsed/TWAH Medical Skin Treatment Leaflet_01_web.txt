What is Botulinum Toxin?
甚麼是肉毒桿菌素？
Botulinum Toxin has been used for therapeutic purposes to treat 
strabismus and spasm since 1980s. Subsequently, its cosmetic use was 
ﬁrst approved by US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in 2002. 
自八十年代起，肉毒桿菌素已用於治療斜視和痙攣，其後並於
2002年首次獲得美國食品及藥物管理局 (FDA) 美容方面的認
證。
Application of Botulinum Toxin Technology
肉毒桿菌素技術的應用
Usually 3 to 5 injection points will be chosen carefully in each treatment 
area and tiny stinging sensation may be felt during injection through 
an ultra-ﬁne needle. Pre- and post-injection ice therapy will minimize 
the injection sensation. 
The duration of effect is variable among different users. In general, it 
could last up to 3 to 4 months but may last longer, up to 6 months, 
for regular users. 
一般而言，針對每個治療範圍，醫生會選擇三至五個部位，以
特細針頭注射，期間可能感覺輕微的刺痛。不過，只需要於注
射前和注射後冰敷，便可減少刺痛感。
因應不同使用者，其療效可維持的時間亦有所區別，通常可持
續三至四個月，對定期使用者來說，效果可以長達半年。
Wrinkles form due to the development of tense muscles under the 
skin and the collagen depletion there. Botulinum Toxin injections can 
relax such tense muscles and smoothen the wrinkles leading to a more 
youthful appearance.
皺紋源於皮膚下的肌肉拉緊，及膠原蛋白損耗。肉毒桿菌素可
放鬆拉緊的肌肉，撫平皺紋，令外貌看起來較年輕。
Wrinkle Treatment (Upper, Lower Face and Neck)
除皺（上半面、下半面和頸部）
Botulinum Toxin treatments can relax chewing muscles which will slim 
faces. Temporarily, users may not be able to chew very hard food. By 
relaxing the depressor muscles through Botulinum Toxin injections, 
full face contouring can be achieved because of the "lifting" effect 
given by the remaining untreated elevator muscle. 
注射後可以令臉頰兩側的咀嚼肌放鬆，令臉部看起來較纖瘦，
但接受注射人士暫時可能無法咀嚼非常堅硬的食物。
肉毒桿菌素又可以放鬆面部降肌，減弱向下的拉力，配合面部
提肌本身提升的作用，就能改善面部輪廓。
Face Slimming and Full Face Contouring
瘦面及改善面部輪廓
Botulinum Toxin can effectively treat excessive sweating by suppressing 
sweat glands which will inhibit hand sweating and underarm sweating, 
thus signiﬁcantly improving the social life of patients.
利用肉毒桿菌素抑制汗腺，可以壓制手汗和腋下出汗，有效治
療多汗症，大大改善多汗症患者的社交生活。
