Reference Charges for Common Surgical Procedures
The information listed below is derived from the data of standard room charges of the hospital in the year of 2025. The information on this list are for reference only and will be updated regularly. Prices are subject to change 
based on the complexity of each individual case and the corresponding fees charged by the doctor. 
以下資料取自本院普通病房2025年的收費紀錄，所有數據只作參考用途，實際費用須視乎病情複雜性及個別醫生收費而定。此列表會定期更新。
1. The information provided above is based on data for standard ward accomodation and individual procedure. Data for multiple procedures are excluded. 
2. Doctor's fees include fees for anaesthesiologist, operating surgeon and ward round. 
3. Hospital charges include the cost of accommodation, admission, surgical supplies, nursing care, investigation and examination, medication, meals, sundries, etc. No admission fee is charged. 
Remarks
1.以上為病人進行單一手術及入住普通房間費用的統計資料。同時進行兩項或以上手術的有關資料並未納入統計範圍。
2.醫生費包括相關程序的醫生費、麻醉科專科醫生費，及醫生巡房費。
3.醫院費包括登記入住費、住宿費、手術消耗品及相關物料、護理費、檢驗和檢查費，及藥物、餐膳、飲料和一般雜項的費用。本院不設入院費。
