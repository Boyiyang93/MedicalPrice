Remarks 
The above charges only include the operating room fee. All medication, consumable supplies, special equipment and instruments are not included. 
Office hours of operating room: Monday to Thursday, 08:00 - 22:00; Friday and Sunday, 08:00 - 18:00. Non-office hours of operating room / emergency services: Saturday and Hospital holiday. 
A 50% emergency surcharge will be levied for services during the non-office hours of operating room / emergency services or when scheduled with less than 4 hours notice. 
The above prices are for your reference. There may be further details that are not included here. Please re-confirm the prices with our staff prior to receiving treatments or examinations. 
Hong Kong Adventist Hospital - Stubbs Road reserves the right to update the fee schedule, brochure, terms and conditions. Any change of fee schedule will be announced and notices will be published in advance, in accordance with the statutory notice period. Any other changes except the fee schedule may be made at any time as it sees fit without prior notice, you are advised to check the latest information before using our services.
