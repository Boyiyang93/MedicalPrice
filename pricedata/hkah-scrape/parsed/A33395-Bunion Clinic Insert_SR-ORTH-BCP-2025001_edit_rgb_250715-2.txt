Bunion Clinic Packages  
A bunion, also known as hallux valgus or bursitis, is a 
condition that affects many people. To provide 
comprehensive care to those in need, the Bunion 
Clinic at Hong Kong Adventist Hospital – Stubbs 
Road offers packages that cover surgery for bunion 
correction and post-operative follow-up care.
In particular, the Bunion Surgery Hospital Care 
Package includes not only hospital expenses related 
physiotherapist and gait training during the patient’s 
hospital stay as well. A bunion shoe and a pair of 
crutches are also provided.
Is Surgery Necessary for
Bunion Correction? 
Bunions may be treated via non-surgical and surgical 
methods, the latter of which encompasses various 
treatment options. With the continued advancement 
of medical technology, minimally invasive surgery 
may  now be used to treat bunions, although this 
method is not suitable for all cases.  Those with 
severe deformity or have bunions that involve 
multiple toes, for example, may not qualify for 
minimally invasive surgery.
As we rely heavily on the strength of our big toe 
when walking, bunions not only affect the 
appearance of our foot and cause pain, but may also 
impact our ability to walk normally. It is therefore 
important to consult a specialist early for 
examination and diagnosis so as to determine the 
appropriate treatment as soon as possible.
拇趾外翻診所套餐
拇趾外翻又稱為拇趾滑液囊炎，是
令不少人深受困擾的疾病。香港  
港安醫院─司徒拔道的拇趾外翻診
所特別推出拇指外翻手術套餐及術
後門診套餐，為有需要人士提供全
方位照顧。
其中，拇趾外翻手術套餐除包括有
關手術的醫院費用外，更涵蓋住院
期間的物理治療師評估和步行訓   
練，及康復期間需穿着的拇趾外翻
專用鞋及拐杖。
拇趾外翻必須通過手術
治療嗎？
治療拇趾外翻包括非手術和手術治
療，後者更有多種治療方式。隨着
醫療科技發展，現時已可使用微創
手術治療拇趾外翻，只是並非所有
情況適用，例如拇趾嚴重變形、  
多隻腳趾變形等。
我們行走時，十分倚重拇趾的力   
量，故拇趾外翻不單影響外觀、帶
來痛楚，連日常行走都有機會受影
響。因為，及早接受專科醫生檢查
和診斷，以決定治療方式，便尤為
重要。
