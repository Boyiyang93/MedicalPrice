Shoulder Care Packages  
Stiﬀ, painful shoulders are a common issue 
amongst busy urbanites. Although symptoms may 
initially be minor or imperceptible, they may be an 
early indication of physical fatigue and could lead 
to serious consequences if left untreated. Patients 
should seek medical attention as soon as possible.
Hong Kong Adventist Hospital – Stubbs Road’s 
Shoulder Care Packages are specially designed to 
provide patients with a wide range of services 
including diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, 
minimally invasive surgery, and joint replacement 
surgery. The packages are managed by a team of 
shoulder expert and medical professionals, who are 
committed to oﬀering comprehensive care to each 
patient on his/her journey of recovery.
What is Shoulder Arthroscopy?
Shoulder arthroscopy is a minimally invasive 
surgery where a tiny camera (an arthroscope) is 
inserted through a small incision in the skin to 
examine or repair the tissues inside or around the 
shoulder joint. Other small surgical instruments 
may also be used during the procedure to repair 
the 
joint 
or 
conduct 
other 
arthroscopic 
procedures.*
