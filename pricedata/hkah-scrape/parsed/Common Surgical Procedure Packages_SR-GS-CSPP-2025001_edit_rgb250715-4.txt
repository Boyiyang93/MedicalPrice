Common Surgical Procedure Packages  
Hong Kong Adventist Hospital – Stubbs Road offers 
Hospital Care Packages for various common 
surgical procedures. Patients receive high-quality 
and autonomy as they may choose between private 
(single), semi-private (double), and standard rooms 
(triple or quadruple), as well as their preferred 
specialist in General Surgery.
Package fees include the relevant treatment costs 
accommodation, providing patients with a clear 
understanding of the expected cost of treatment 
prior to receiving care.
Laparoscopic Surgery for Hernia Repair 
Hernia usually forms around the groin, navel, or 
surgical incision and can cause intestinal necrosis. 
This minimally invasive surgery pushes the 
protruding organs back into the abdomen and 
covers the hernia gap with a mesh to repair the 
hernia and prevent further complications.
Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy
During a laparoscopic cholecystectomy, the doctor 
makes a few small incisions in the patient's 
abdomen, inserts an endoscope and surgical 
instruments, and removes the gallbladder. The 
minimally invasive approach results in smaller 
wounds and less blood loss, reducing pain and the 
risk of infection for patients. Typically, patients are 
discharged from the hospital within two days of the 
procedure.
Hemorrhoidectomy
Suffering from hemorrhoids can severely affect 
one's quality of life. If the condition persists or 
worsens, the doctor may recommend surgical 
removal of the hemorrhoids. Conventional surgery 
has a high success rate and a low risk of 
complications. In addition, the implantation of 
surgical staples connects the tissue after the 
hemorrhoid is removed, promoting wound 
healing.

微創疝氣（小腸氣）縫合術
疝氣即小腸氣，通常會在腹股溝、臍
周圍或手術切口形成，可引致腸壞  
死。通過此微創手術，可以將突出器
官推回腹部，纖維網會覆蓋疝氣缺損
的位置，修補疝氣，以防止進一步的
併發症發生。
微創膽囊切除術
這微創手術是在患者腹部刺穿幾個小
切口，將內窺鏡及手術儀器放入體 
內，醫生便能透過屏幕切除膽囊。好
處是傷口細及失血少，減低患者的痛
楚及傷口受感染風險，病人一般可在
手術後兩天內出院。
痔瘡切除術
患上痔瘡可能會嚴重影響生活，如果
病情持續或愈來愈嚴重，醫生可能會
建議通過手術切除痔瘡。據統計，傳
統方式的痔瘡切除術能治癒大部份個
案，而且併發症出現的機率低。植入
式縫合釘則是在切除痔瘡後，利用縫
合釘把組織連接在一起，有助促進傷
口痊癒。
