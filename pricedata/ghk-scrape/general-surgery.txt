TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF THE PACKAGE 套餐使用條款: 
 Doctor reserves the right to decide the suitability and risk level of package according to individual 
patient's condition upon assessment in a consultation. 醫生與病人會診後，可根據病人之情況評估風險及
決定病人是否適合接受套餐。 
 The package applies only to patients staying in standard room, and expires upon discharge. 此套餐只適
用於入住標準房，並於病人出院時失效。 
 There will not be any refund for discharge earlier than the end date of the package. 於套餐完結日前提早
出院將不獲任何退款。 
 If the patient is diagnosed with a disease which deviates from the original diagnosis and requires a 
different course of treatment subsequent to admission to hospital, the hospital may void the package and 
charge according to the new treatment required. In that case, patients (or their next-of-kin / authorised 
persons) will be informed of the new charges with consent sought before the new course of treatment is 
administered. 如病人入院後被診斷的病因與原來不同，並需要接受另一項治療，醫院可取消有關套餐，並
收取新的治療所需之費用。在病人接受新的治療前，病人或其親屬或獲授權人士將會獲事先告知新的收
費，醫院亦會先徵得其同意後才作進一步治療。 
 All prices are subject to change depending on the actual circumstances. 所有收費將視乎實際情況而有所
改變。 
 Gleneagles Hospital Hong Kong reserves the right of final decision on the suitability and risk level of 
package. 港怡醫院保留套餐風險評估及病人適用與否之最終決定權。
