1. The above package includes a 6-hour hospital stay. A surcharge of $210 per hour will be charged if any additional hours 
are required.  
2. Doctor reserves the right to decide the suitability of the above Package Fee according to individual patient’s condition. 
3. Additional charges for other therapeutic procedures may be incurred, e.g. removal of foreign body, haemostasis, rubber          
band ligation of haemorrhoids, pathology, etc. 
4. If the patient is diagnosed with a medical condition different from his or her original diagnosis after admission or in 
the course of the procedures and requires alternative treatment and/or services, the hospital may void the package and 
charge according to the new treatment required.  
5. Evangel Hospital reserves the right to amend any of the above terms and conditions without prior notice. In case of any 
dispute, the decision of Evangel Hospital shall be final and conclusive.
