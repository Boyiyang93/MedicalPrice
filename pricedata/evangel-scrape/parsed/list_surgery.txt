Remarks:
(1) The above information is based on the data from patients accommodating in general ward and undergoing a single procedure. 
      Data from patients undergoing multiple procedures are excluded.  Some other minor treatments with endoscopy may be included.
(2) Doctor's fee includes fees for anaesthetist, operating surgeon and ward round, etc.
(3) Hospital charges include admission fee, room charge, operating theatre charge, treatment and associated material charges, 
      nursing procedure fees, investigation and examination fees, medication and injection fees, meal and beverage charges, sundries 
      charges, etc.
(4) Information above is for reference only. The exact charges would be subject to change in accordance with patient’s 
      condition, case complexity and individual doctor’s charge incurred.
(5) We do not provide such services.
(6) The number of services of certain procedures were minimal that it may not reveal the clear picture of charging fees.
備註:
(1) 上述資料只包括入住普通病房及接受單一項手術的統計數字，並不包括同時進行多項手術的資料。在有關內視鏡的資料中
     會包括一些額外治療的收費。
(2) 醫生費內包含麻醉師費，醫生手術費及巡房費等。
(3) 醫院費內包括入院費、病房租金、手術室租金、治療及有關物料費、護理程序、化驗及檢查費、藥費及藥物注射費
      、膳食及雜項收費等。
(4) 以上統計資料只供參考。各項目的實際收費會根據病人的情況、手術的複雜性及個別醫生之收費等而有所改變。
(5) 本院暫時未有提供該項服務。
(6) 部分手術個案因宗數太少，未必能反映的實際情況。
