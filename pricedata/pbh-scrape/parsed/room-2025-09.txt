註： 
1. 每位入院病人均須繳付入院登記費。 
2. 所有房間均設有無線上網服務。 
3. 房租不包括手術室費、藥費、膳食費、化驗及各類治療或檢查費用。 
4. 病人需自行與醫生協定有關醫生及麻醉科醫生等費用。 
5. 如需在正常服務時間以外進行化驗、檢驗或手術，本院會收取較高的費用，詳細收費可向醫院職員查詢。 
6. 住院費用按照病人選擇房間類別釐定收費，如住院期間轉較高類別房間，本院將會由入院當日起收取差額。 
7. 床位類別之分配須視乎入院當天病床供應而定，本院有權調遷客人之房間或床位。 
8. 如病人要求轉房/床，每次轉換須繳付額外之手續費 HK$500。 
9. 醫院賬項一般每三天結算一次。有關付款方式，請參閱本院網站或繳費處告示。 
10. 病人出院結賬時，煩請出示按金收據，否則本院有權拒絕退還按金。

Remarks： 
1. Admission service fee is charged for every in-patient. 
2. Free Wi-Fi broadband internet service is available. 
3. Room charges do not include other service fee, doctor’s consultation, surgical operation, anesthesia fees etc. 
4. Patients are advised to consult their attending doctors regarding their fees before admission. 
5. All emergency examinations and procedures done in non-service hours will be charged at a higher rate. Please contact our staff for details. 
6. Bed charge is levied according to the patient’s admitted room category. All service charges incurred on the day of admission are 
retrospectively charged at the highest rate of the bed stayed in. (including expenses in the Operating Theatre). 
7. The bed category assigned will be subject to bed availability on the day of admission; the Hospital reserves the right to make 
changes of room/bed for patients as needed. 
8. An administrative fee of $500 is charged for each request for changing bed/ward. 
9. Hospital Charges are generally payable every 3 days. Please refer to hospital website or cashier notice for payment method. 
10. Please provide the deposit receipt upon discharge, as the hospital reserves the right not to refund the deposit without the original receipt.
