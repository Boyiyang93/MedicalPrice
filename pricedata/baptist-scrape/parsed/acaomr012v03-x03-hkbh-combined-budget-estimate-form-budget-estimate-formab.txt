Figures listed are derived from statistics of actual discharge bills of relevant patients who underwent similar treatment in our hospital 
last year and the preliminary treatment items chosen by the doctor. Doctors’ management (e.g. choice of procedures, drugs and 
consumables) of the same illness may differ.
