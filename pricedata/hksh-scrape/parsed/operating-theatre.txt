Operating Theatre Room Charge — Hong Kong Sanatorium & Hospital
Effective from 1 August 2024
Source: images/ot-charges.jpg

Level 1 (up to 15 mins):
  Outpatient/General Ward: HK$1,950 (extra 15m HK$1,180)
  Semi-Private: HK$2,540 (extra 15m HK$1,530)
  Private/Suite: HK$3,120 (extra 15m HK$1,890)

Level 4 (up to 60 mins):
  Outpatient/General Ward: HK$8,510 (extra 15m HK$1,380)
  Semi-Private: HK$11,060 (extra 15m HK$1,790)
  Private/Suite: HK$13,620 (extra 15m HK$2,210)

Level 7 (up to 240 mins):
  Outpatient/General Ward: HK$51,240
  Semi-Private: HK$66,610
  Private/Suite: HK$81,980

Recovery Care (after 1st hour): General HK$230 / Semi HK$340 / Private HK$460 per 30 mins.
Charges exclude equipment, instruments, medications, consumables.
