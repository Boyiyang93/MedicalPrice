Well Women Screening Programmes
Health is a top priority for women juggling work and 
home responsibilities. At HKSH, the Women's Health 
Clinic sets out to optimise the health of all women and 
address their needs at every stage of life. With routine 
screenings and other options for all age groups, health 
problems can be detected at an early stage for timely 
diagnosis and effective follow-up.
	•
Breast Examination and Pap Smear
Breast cancer and cervical cancer are common 
among women. Early detection through regular 
screenings can lead to prompt treatment and 
improve outcomes. 
	•
Bone Densitometry
Osteoporosis is common in post-menopausal 
women. If undetected, it may contribute to frequent 
fractures after minor accidents or trauma. Early 
detection of bone loss can facilitate prompt 
treatment, restore lost bone mass, and reduce the 
chance of fractures. 
Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DEXA) is used to 
assess osteroporosis by measuring the bone density 
of major bones such as the spine and femur. Its 
radiation dose is only one-tenth of that of chest X-ray.
